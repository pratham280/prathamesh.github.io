# 🧗 Inverted Rope Climber Robot – IIT Roorkee Techfest

A custom **inverted rope climbing robot** developed for **IIT Roorkee’s Techfest**.  
The robot was designed to traverse along suspended ropes using a **four-bar linkage mechanism** that converts rotational motion into oscillatory arm movement.  

---

## ⚙️ Design & Development
- **CAD Software**: Fusion 360 (mechanical design & motion validation)  
- **Fabrication**: All components 3D printed in **PLA material** using **Bambu Lab A1 Mini** printer  
- **Drive Motor**: 100 RPM BO motor for reliable and lightweight actuation  
- **Gear Train**: Custom gear system designed to convert motor’s rotary motion into oscillating arm motion  
- **Mechanism**: 4-bar linkage for synchronized and stable climbing movement  

---

## 🤖 Working Principle
- The robot uses **two arms**, positioned **180° out of phase**, to grip and release the rope alternately.  
- As the BO motor rotates, the **gear drivetrain** powers the 4-bar linkage, causing the arms to oscillate.  
- This out-of-phase motion ensures continuous forward climbing along the rope.  

---

## 📐 Key Specifications
- **Motor**: 100 RPM BO motor  
- **Mechanism**: 4-bar linkage with gear drivetrain  
- **Material**: PLA (3D printed)  
- **Fabrication Method**: FDM printing on **Bambu Lab A1 Mini**  
- **Motion**: Alternating arm oscillation, providing forward climbing movement  

---

## 🚀 Outcome
- Successfully demonstrated **rope climbing in inverted orientation**.  
- Achieved smooth, continuous motion using **oscillating linkage arms**.  
- Lightweight and fully 3D-printed design made it compact and competition-ready.  

---

## 📌 Significance
This project showcased the **application of four-bar linkage mechanics** in robotics, blending **kinematic design, 3D printing, and motor-driven actuation**.  
It served as a proof-of-concept for lightweight, cost-effective climbing robots in task-based competitions.
