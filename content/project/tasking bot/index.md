# 🚙 4WD Tasking Bot – IIT Bombay Techfest

A custom-built **4WD robotic platform** designed for task-oriented challenges at **IIT Bombay’s Techfest**.  
This bot was engineered with robust components for speed, stability, and reliable control in competitive environments.  

---

## ⚙️ Specifications

- 🔋 **Power System**: 3S Li-Po battery (11.1V nominal)  
- 🏎️ **Drive Motors**: 4× 348 RPM brushed DC motors  
- ⚡ **Motor Driver**: 15D motor driver (high current support)  
- 🖲️ **Control System**: ExpressLRS-based Radiomaster remote controller  
- 🛞 **Wheels**: Custom rubber wheels for improved traction and handling  
- 🛠️ **Chassis**: Lightweight aluminum frame for durability and reduced weight  

---

## 🎮 Control & Operation
- Controlled wirelessly via **ExpressLRS Radiomaster** remote for **low latency** and high range.  
- Independent motor control enables precise maneuverability.  
- Optimized for **task completion, navigation, and obstacle handling**.  

---

## 🚀 Applications & Capabilities
- **Competition Ready**: Designed for task-based challenges in Techfest.  
- **Agility & Speed**: High-RPM motors allow quick response and rapid traversal.  
- **Durability**: Aluminum chassis + rubber wheels offer ruggedness on uneven surfaces.  
- **Expandability**: Can be upgraded with sensors for autonomous navigation (ROS integration, LiDAR, cameras, etc.).
