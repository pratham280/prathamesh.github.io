# 🌀 EDF (Electric Ducted Fan) Development & Prototyping

This project involved the design, analysis, and prototyping of an **Electric Ducted Fan (EDF)** to enhance the performance of a line follower robot by creating **downforce for improved traction**.  

---

## ⚙️ Design & Development
- **Software Used**: Fusion 360 (CAD design & assembly)  
- **Components Designed**:  
  - Duct (aerodynamically shaped for efficient airflow)  
  - Fan (optimized blade geometry)  
  - Mounting structure (to integrate EDF with robot chassis)  
- **Assembly**: All parts were designed, validated, and integrated in Fusion 360 before fabrication.  

---

## 🌬️ Fluid Dynamics Analysis
- Conducted **CFD (Computational Fluid Dynamics)** simulations in Fusion 360.  
- Analyzed airflow patterns, pressure distribution, and generated thrust.  
- Optimized duct shape to minimize turbulence and maximize efficiency.  
- Verified that thrust levels were sufficient to **increase ground adhesion** of the line follower bot.  

---

## 🔋 Hardware & Specifications
- **Motor**: 6000KV mini BLDC motor  
- **Power Source**: 2S Li-Po battery (7.4V nominal)  
- **Estimated RPM**: ~44,400 RPM (6000 × 7.4)  
- **Fabrication**: Components 3D printed in **PETG plastic** using **Bambu Lab A1 Mini** printer  
- **Use Case**: Mount on the chassis of the line follower bot to provide **downward thrust**, improving traction and stability during high-speed turns.  

---

## 🚀 Outcome
- Successfully designed, fabricated, and assembled a **custom EDF system**.  
- Achieved simulated thrust values sufficient for creating **aerodynamic downforce**.  
- Provided the line follower bot with **better traction, reduced slippage, and improved performance** in competitive conditions.  

---

## 📌 Significance
This project showcased the **integration of aerodynamics and robotics**, applying **fluid dynamics analysis, CAD design, additive manufacturing, and high-speed BLDC technology** to enhance robotic mobility.  
It served as a proof of concept for using EDFs in small-scale robotics applications.
